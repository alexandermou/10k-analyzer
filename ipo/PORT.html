What the company does

Southport Acquisition Corp. II is a blank-check company (SPAC). It raises cash from investors, holds it in a trust account, and then looks for a private company to merge with, which takes that company public. It says it will focus on artificial intelligence.

Things to weigh

  • No operating business yet: the figures that matter come later, with the merger target it picks.
  • Shareholders can usually redeem their shares for the cash in trust when a merger is put to a vote.
