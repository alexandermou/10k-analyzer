Use of proceeds, per the filing: general corporate purposes (working capital, operating expenses, capital expenditures), and possibly acquisitions. No dividends planned.

What the company does

vVARDIS sells Curodont, a peptide-based liquid applied to early-stage cavities. The company says it helps stop the decay and rebuild enamel without drilling, in a treatment of up to five minutes.

  • U.S. launch: January 2024. In the U.S. it's sold as an over-the-counter drug because of its fluoride; the filing notes the FDA hasn't ruled on its safety or efficacy.
  • Sold to over 20,000 of about 110,000 U.S. general dental practices, and used on over 3.5 million teeth by June 30, 2026 (company estimates).
  • The company puts its U.S. addressable market at about $29 billion (its own estimate).
